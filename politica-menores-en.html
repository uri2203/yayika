Minors Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Commitment to Protecting Minors

At Yayika, the safety of minors is a fundamental priority. This policy establishes the measures we implement to protect minors who may interact with our platform, in accordance with applicable international legislation.

Yayika is a platform designed exclusively for women over 18 years old. It is not intended for, nor designed to be used by minors.

2. Minimum Age Requirement

Use of Yayika is strictly limited to persons 18 years of age or older.

If it is discovered that a minor under 18 has created an account or is using the platform without authorization:

  • The account will be immediately suspended.
  • All associated data will be deleted from our systems within a maximum of 72 hours.
  • Parents or legal guardians will be notified if contact information is available.
  • An incident report will be filed in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

3. Parental or Guardian Consent

If a person between 14 and 17 years old wishes to use the platform with parental or guardian consent, they must:

  • Submit a formal request to admin@yayika.com.
  • Include authorization signed by the parent, mother, or legal guardian.
  • Provide official identification for both the guardian and the minor.
  • Receive express approval from the Yayika team before creating any account.

Approval is not guaranteed and will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

4. COPPA Compliance — United States

🇺🇸 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

For users residing in the United States, Yayika fully complies with COPPA:

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13.
  • If it is discovered that a user under 13 provided data, it will be immediately deleted.
  • Parents may request deletion of their child's data by contacting admin@yayika.com.
  • We do not use behavioral advertising targeted at minors.
  • We maintain age verification records in accordance with COPPA regulations.

5. Digital Services — European Union

🇪🇺 Digital Services Act + GDPR Art. 8

In compliance with the Digital Services Act (DSA) and GDPR:

  • We do not offer services directly to minors under 16 (or the age established by the Member State).
  • Consent for minors under 16 requires authorization from the holder of parental authority (Art. 8 GDPR).
  • We implement robust age verification.
  • We do not engage in profiling of minors.
  • We provide easy mechanisms for account and data deletion.

6. National Legislation by Country

🇲🇽 Mexico — Ley General de los Derechos de Niñas, Niños y Adolescentes

Yayika complies with the General Law on the Rights of Girls, Boys and Adolescents (LGDNNA) and the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data:

  • The best interests of the child are protected in all our operations.
  • Minors under 18 require parental or guardian consent.
  • We respect the right to identity and privacy of minors.
  • We cooperate with authorities in cases of detected exploitation or abuse.

🇧🇷 Brazil — LGPD Art. 14 + Estatuto da Criança e do Adolescente

For users residing in Brazil, we comply with the General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and the Child and Adolescent Statute (ECA):

  • Art. 14 LGPD: Processing of minors' data may only be carried out with specific and highlighted consent from at least one parent or guardian.
  • We do not offer services to minors under 12 without parental consent.
  • Between 12 and 18 years old, parental consent is required.
  • Enhanced protection measures apply to minors' data.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom — UK GDPR + Age Appropriate Design Code

  • We comply with the UK GDPR and the Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code).
  • We do not offer services to minors under 13.
  • Between 13 and 17, verified parental consent is required.
  • We apply the 15 principles of the Children's Code.
  • We implement privacy-by-default settings for minors.

🇨🇦 Canada — PIPEDA + Provincial Laws

  • We comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).
  • We respect provincial data protection laws (such as FIPPA in British Columbia).
  • We obtain meaningful consent before collecting data from minors under 13.
  • Parents may request access to and deletion of their child's data.

8. Technical Protection Measures

  • Age verification at registration.
  • Inappropriate content filters.
  • Active reporting and moderation system.
  • Encryption of sensitive data.
  • Restricted staff access to minors' data.
  • Periodic compliance audits.

9. Reports and Complaints

If you suspect that a minor is being exploited or is at risk on our platform:

  • Use the "Report" button in the application.
  • Send an email to admin@yayika.com with the subject "URGENT — Minor at risk".
  • Contact local child protection authorities.
  • In emergencies, call your country's emergency number.
