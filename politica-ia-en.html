AI Usage Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Commitment to AI Transparency

At Yayika, we use artificial intelligence to enhance our users' experience. This policy clearly and transparently explains how we use AI, what data we process, and what rights you have as a user.

Fundamental principle: AI at Yayika is a supportive tool. It never replaces qualified medical, financial, or professional advice.

2. How We Use AI at Yayika

Currently, AI systems are used in the following functionalities:

FunctionDescriptionData Used
Chat assistant Answering frequently asked questions and providing general platform guidance Conversation history (temporary)
Cycle intelligence Analysis of cycle patterns to provide educational information Cycle data entered by the user
Content recommendations Suggesting articles, resources, and relevant content Preferences and browsing history

3. Transparency: Knowing When You Interact with AI

We believe in full transparency. Therefore:

  • Explicit notification: You will always be informed when you are interacting with an AI system.
  • Visible labeling: AI-generated responses will carry a clear label such as "AI-generated response" or similar.
  • Communicated limitations: You will be informed about AI limitations and when it is advisable to seek human assistance.

4. Right to Disable AI Features

You have the right to opt out of using AI features. You can:

  • Disable the AI chat assistant from your account settings.
  • Exclude your data from AI processing for recommendations.
  • Request human review of any AI-based suggestion.
  • Contact admin@yayika.com to exercise this right.

5. Automated Decisions and Legal Effects

Guarantee: Yayika does not make automated decisions that produce legal or significant effects on our users. No AI decides for you on medical, financial, or legal matters.

If automated decision-making systems were to be implemented in the future:

  • You will be informed in advance.
  • You will have the right to request human intervention.
  • You will be able to challenge the decision.
  • A prior impact assessment will be conducted.

6. Data Protection and Privacy

Data used by our AI systems is processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Specifically:

  • We do not sell personal data to third parties for AI training.
  • We do not share your data with external AI providers for their own models.
  • Cycle data is processed in pseudonymized form.
  • Encryption and anonymization technical measures are applied.
  • You can request deletion of your AI-processed data at any time.

7. AI Capabilities and Limitations

For your complete information:

Help navigate the platformMake financial or legal decisions for you

8. Consent and Control

AI use at Yayika is based on your consent:

  • During registration, your explicit consent is requested for AI use.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time from settings.
  • Withdrawing consent will not affect basic platform use.
  • We will maintain a record of your consent.

9. Regional Legislation

???? European Union � EU AI Act (effective August 2026)

Yayika fully complies with the European AI Act:

  • Risk classification: Our AI systems are classified as "limited risk" or "minimal risk," with corresponding compliance measures.
  • Transparency: We comply with the information and transparency requirements of Art. 52 of the EU AI Act.
  • Human oversight: We implement effective human oversight in all AI systems.
  • Conformity assessment: We conduct conformity assessments before deployment.
  • Data subject rights: We respect the right to receive information about AI use and to request human intervention.

???? Mexico � Regulatory Framework

In Mexico, there is no specific AI law yet, but initiatives are being discussed in Congress:

  • We comply with the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties.
  • We apply the principles of lawfulness, consent, information, quality, purpose, loyalty, proportionality, and responsibility.
  • We closely follow legislative initiatives on AI in the Chamber of Deputies.
  • We implement international best practices for AI governance.

???? Brazil � LGPD Art. 20

For users in Brazil, we comply with the LGPD regarding automated decisions:

  • Art. 20 LGPD: You have the right to request a review of decisions made solely based on automated processing of personal data.
  • We will provide clear information about the criteria used in automated decisions.
  • We offer mechanisms to request human intervention.
  • We maintain records of automated decisions made.

???? United States � State-Level Legislation

In the US, there is no comprehensive federal AI law, but we implement best practices:

  • We comply with the CCPA/CPRA of California regarding automated decisions.
  • We respect the Colorado AI Act and emerging state legislation.
  • We implement non-discrimination measures in algorithms.
  • We conduct algorithmic bias impact assessments.

???? Canada � Automated Decision-Making Directive

  • We comply with the Government of Canada's Directive on Automated Decision-Making.
  • We implement meaningful human oversight in AI systems.
  • We conduct algorithmic impact assessments.
  • We respect the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

???? Switzerland � Federal AI Strategy

  • We comply with Swiss data protection legislation (nDSG).
  • We follow the principles of the Federal Artificial Intelligence Strategy.
  • We apply the guidelines of the FDPIC (Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner).
  • We implement AI governance according to Swiss best practices.

10. Assessment and Auditing

  • We conduct periodic assessments of our AI systems.
  • We contract independent compliance audits.
  • We continuously monitor algorithmic bias.
  • We update our systems as best practices evolve.
  • We maintain a record of versions and changes to our AI systems.

11. Updates to This Policy

This policy will be updated as AI regulation and our practices evolve. We will notify you of significant changes through the platform or by email.
