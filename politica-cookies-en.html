Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on Yayika

Last updated: August 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us improve your experience, remember your preferences, and analyze how the platform is used.

2. Types of cookies we use

2.1 Essential cookies

Necessary for the basic functioning of the platform. Without these cookies, Yayika cannot operate correctly.

CookiePurposeDuration
session_idKeep your session activeSession

2.2 Analytics cookies

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics tool. Plausible does not use cookies and does not track individual users. It complies with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR without requiring a consent banner for analytics.

2.3 Preference cookies

Remember your personal settings to offer you a better experience.

CookiePurposeDuration
themeLight/dark theme1 year
languageSelected language1 year

2.4 Marketing cookies

We do not currently use marketing cookies. If implemented in the future, your explicit consent will be requested before activation.

3. Cookie consent

When you first visit Yayika, a cookie banner will be displayed where you can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Customize your preferences

You can change your preferences at any time from your account settings or by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.

4. How to manage cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Note that disabling certain cookies may affect platform functionality.

5. Regulatory compliance

🇪🇺 European Union — ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC

We comply with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Directive. We request consent before storing non-essential cookies on your device. Cookies strictly necessary for the requested service are exempt from consent.

🇧🇷 Brazil — LGPD and ANPD guidelines

In compliance with the General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and the guidelines of the National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), we provide transparency about cookie usage and offer easy opt-in/opt-out mechanisms.

🇺🇸 United States — CCPA/CPRA

In accordance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to opt out of the sale of personal information collected through cookies. Yayika does not sell personal data.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom — PECR

We comply with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). Non-essential cookies require explicit consent before installation.

6. Third-party cookies

We currently do not use third-party cookies for advertising or tracking. Our only analytics service (Plausible) does not use cookies.

7. Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to update this cookie policy. Significant changes will be communicated through the cookie banner and by email to registered users.

8. Contact

If you have questions about our cookie policy, contact us at cookies@yayika.com.
