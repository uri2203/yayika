Community Policy

The values that unite us at Yayika

Last updated: August 2026

1. Yayika is a community, NOT a marketplace

Yayika is a space designed to connect, empower, and support women entrepreneurs. We are not a marketplace or an e-commerce platform. We facilitate connections, but transactions, payments, and business agreements are managed directly between sellers and buyers.

Sellers are responsible for:

  • Managing their own payments and collections
  • Complying with their fiscal and tax obligations
  • Setting their own prices and sales conditions
  • Managing shipping, returns, and customer service

2. Our core values

Respect

We treat all people with dignity and consideration, regardless of their origin, orientation, identity, or abilities.

Empowerment

We support each other to grow, learn, and achieve our goals as women entrepreneurs.

Support

We build a solidarity network where everyone can find help, advice, and companionship.

Inclusion

We celebrate diversity and create a safe space for all women, without exception.

3. Prohibited behaviors

Yayika has zero tolerance for the following behaviors:

Harassment and gender-based violence

We do not tolerate any form of harassment, intimidation, threats, or gender-based violence. Machismo, sexism, and gender discrimination are strictly prohibited.

Hate speech and discrimination

Any content or behavior that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence against any person or group based on race, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, or any other condition is prohibited.

Spam and scams

Sending unsolicited content, deceptive advertising, links to malicious websites, or any fraudulent activity is not permitted.

Inappropriate content

Sharing sexually explicit, violent content, or content that promotes illegal activities is prohibited.

Impersonation

Impersonating another person, brand, or entity, or providing false or misleading information is not allowed.

4. Content moderation

Yayika reserves the right to:

  • Review, moderate, or remove content that violates this policy
  • Suspend or delete accounts that violate community rules
  • Report illegal activities to the relevant authorities
  • Update these rules as necessary to protect the community

5. Reporting system

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this policy:

  • Use the "Report" button available on each profile and post
  • Send an email to comunidad@yayika.com
  • Your identity will be kept confidential and will not be shared with the reported person

All reports will be reviewed by our moderation team within a maximum of 48 hours.

6. Sanctions

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Warning: Formal notification of improper conduct
  • Temporary restriction: Limitation of account functions for a determined period
  • Suspension: Temporary account lock
  • Removal: Permanent account deletion in serious or repeat cases

7. Your responsibilities

By joining Yayika, you commit to:

  • Respect all community members
  • Report inappropriate behavior instead of responding aggressively
  • Maintain the confidentiality of other people's private information
  • Cooperate with the moderation team when requested

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at comunidad@yayika.com.
